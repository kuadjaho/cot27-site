Speakers & guests

Keynote speakers, district officers and guests of honor of the 2026 Convention.

AD
Keynote speaker

Aïchatou Dossou, DTM

Keynote speaker

Distinguished Toastmaster, leadership coach and founder of three clubs in Benin. She will open the convention with her keynote “Dare to Speak”.

KM
Keynote speaker

Kwame Mensah, DTM

Closing keynote

Regional public speaking champion and entrepreneur, he will share his vision “Build the Future” for West African youth.

EH

Élodie Hounkpatin

District 130 Director

At the head of District 130, she drives the growth of Toastmasters clubs in Benin and the sub-region.

RA

Dr Rachidi Alao

Workshop “Powerful storytelling”

Communication professor and researcher, he has trained executives in the art of storytelling for 15 years.

FD

Fatoumata Diallo

Workshop “Women in leadership”

Division director and mentor, she supports women leaders across French-speaking Africa.

JA

Jean-Baptiste Agossou

Master of ceremonies

A well-known voice of district conventions, he will host the three days with energy and humor.
