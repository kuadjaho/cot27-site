Getting to Cotonou

✈️

By plane

🚌

By road

Formalities

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a Toastmasters member to attend?+

No! The convention is open to everyone. Guests are welcome with the Guest Pass.

How can I pay for my registration?+

Online by Mobile Money (MTN, Moov) or bank card, or in cash at the convention welcome desk.

Do I need a visa to travel to Benin?+

ECOWAS nationals do not need a visa. Other nationalities can get an e-Visa online within 48 h at evisa.gouv.bj.

Is lunch included in the pass?+

Yes, coffee breaks and lunches for all three days are included in the Member, Guest and VIP passes.

Can I get a refund if I can no longer attend?+

Registrations are 100% refundable up to 30 days before the event, then transferable to another attendee.
