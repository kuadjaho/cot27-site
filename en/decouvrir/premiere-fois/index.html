First conference?

Welcome! Here is everything you need to enjoy COT27 from the very first hour — even if you have never set foot in a Toastmasters club.

🎟️01

Choose your pass

Member or not, everyone is welcome. The pass covers keynotes, workshops, contests and networking breaks. The gala and excursions are add-ons.

🗓️02

Build your week

Eight days, with a three-day conference core: arrive for the contests, stay for the gala. Excursions fit before or after.

🤝03

Come for the people

A Toastmasters conference is 450 people cheering your first steps. Nobody stays alone for more than five minutes.

👔04

Pack two outfits

Business casual by day, formal attire for the Saturday gala. May in Cotonou: light, breathable, plus an umbrella.
