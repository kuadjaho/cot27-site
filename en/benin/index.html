Destination Benin

You do not cross half a continent to see only a conference room. Benin is part of the program.

Official excursions

Four stops, one story

🏛️Full day

Ouidah

The memory

The Slave Route down to the Door of No Return, the Python Temple, the History Museum: an emotional day one hour from Cotonou.

🛶Half day

Ganvié

The lagoon

The “Venice of Africa”: a stilt city, floating market and schools on the water, explored by pirogue on Lake Nokoué.

👑Full day

Abomey

The kings

The UNESCO-listed royal palaces of Dahomey: bas-reliefs, thrones and tapestries whose patterns inspire COT27's identity.

🐘2–3 days

Pendjari

The savanna

One of West Africa's last great wildlife sanctuaries: elephants, lions and hippos. A 2–3 day safari extension after the conference.

★ Transport, bilingual guide and lunch included. Limited seats — bookable with your registration.

Planning the trip

Visa, health, currency, weather

🛂

e-Visa in 48 h

ECOWAS nationals: no visa. Others: online e-Visa at evisa.gouv.bj (≈ €50, 48 h processing).

💉

Yellow fever

A yellow fever vaccination card is required on entry. Antimalarial prophylaxis recommended.

💱

CFA franc (XOF)

€1 ≈ 656 FCFA (fixed peg). Cards accepted in hotels; Mobile Money (MTN, Moov) rules everywhere else.

🌦️

May weather

27–31 °C, early rainy season: brief showers, generous sun. Light clothing and an umbrella.

✈️

COO airport

Cardinal Bernardin Gantin International Airport is 20 min from the venue. COT27 shuttles for attendees.

🗣️

Languages

French is the official language, English widely spoken in business — and the conference is fully bilingual.
