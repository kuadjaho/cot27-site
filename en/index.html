The event

Benin's biggest celebration of public speaking

Every year, the District 130 Convention gathers Toastmasters members from Benin and the sub-region for three days of learning, competition and celebration.

Speech contests in French and English, expert-led workshops, awards ceremony, closing gala: live an experience that will transform the way you communicate and lead.

01

District contests

Finals of the humorous speech, table topics and international speech contests.

02

World-class workshops

Hands-on training sessions led by DTMs and international speakers.

03

Gala & awards

A gala evening to celebrate the year's most distinguished clubs and members.

Overview

The heart of the conference

Three intense days, 6–8 May, within a Benin week that starts on the 1st. Excursions take place before or after.
